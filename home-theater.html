FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the consultation process involve? +
We discuss your needs, preferences, and space to create a fully customised plan. Our experts will visit your location to assess acoustic properties and layout requirements at no cost.
How does AI control work for the home theatre? +
HOAI's theatre automation lets you control lights, curtains, sound, and display with a single touch via the mobile app, or with voice commands via Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant — no remote needed.
How long does installation take? +
Installations typically take 2–4 weeks depending on project complexity, room preparation, and equipment availability.
Do you offer maintenance services? +
Yes, we provide comprehensive ongoing support and maintenance to ensure your system continues performing at its best over time.
Which areas do you serve? +
We currently serve Kadapa, Tirupati, Vizag, Hyderabad, Khammam, and Warangal. Contact us to check availability in your area.
Can you work with my existing equipment? +
Absolutely! We can integrate your current setup into a new system, upgrade specific components, or design a completely fresh installation — whatever suits your needs and budget.
